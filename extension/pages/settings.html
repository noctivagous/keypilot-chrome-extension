Overview

Pick a category to change how KeyPilot looks and behaves. Use the list on the left anytime.

Customized

Built-in packs restyle chrome, type, and icons. Edits live on the Appearance tab. Choosing a theme also applies that theme’s Click Mode and cursor defaults.

Reset

Restore all KeyPilot settings to product defaults (appearance, Click Mode, keyboard, scrolling, stored panel positions, and the rest).

Appearance

Customize the selected built-in theme. Changes apply immediately and stay with that pack until you reset or switch themes.

Customized

Switching packs starts from that theme’s defaults (custom edits are cleared). Click Mode defaults follow the pack.

How the hover focus rectangle is drawn. Auto B→C skips DOM outline (A) and uses an in-target ring, then a fixed overlay. Matches Shadow Root Debug (Alt+D).
