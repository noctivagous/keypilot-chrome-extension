# Introduction

KeyPilot is a keyboard-first Chrome extension for navigating and controlling web pages without relying on the mouse.

## Getting started

- Press **Alt+K** to toggle KeyPilot on or off.
- Press **Alt+H** to open this documentation.
- Press **Alt+C** to open Keyboard Layout Config and customize your key map.
- Press **'** (quote) to open Settings.

## What’s here

These docs cover the main parts of KeyPilot: layouts, settings, macros, Execute JS, and more. Topics marked as placeholders will be filled in over time.
