BIMODAL CONTROL

A theory of mouse and keyboard together

The extension exists to promote a UI theory we call Bimodal Control. Steer the cursor with the mouse as before, but activate with keyboard keys instead of mouse buttons. Hover a link and press F to click it, or G to open it in a new background tab. This is fast. It’s called a key-click.

A number of key actions are available besides F. That’s where the power emerges in KeyPilot: a keyboard layout with lots of key-click functions, customizable in the built-in Layout Config editor. It is free and under the MIT License.

The key-click animation on this page shows the gesture: point with the mouse, press F to open, then D to go back.
