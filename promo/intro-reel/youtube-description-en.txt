KeyPilot — The key-click | Keyboard-powered browsing

KeyPilot is a free Chrome extension from Noctivagous Software. The mouse steers. The keyboard activates.

This intro shows the key-click: point with a mouse, press F to open, D to go back. Two inputs + two jobs makes a better interface.
