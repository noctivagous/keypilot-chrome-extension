Harbor lights return to the waterfront this weekend

By Elias Thorne, Gazette Staff Writer
Crews test the new energy-efficient LED filaments on Pier 4 late Thursday evening. Restoration of the historic district is now complete.

PORTLAND — Crews finished the last string of lamps along Pier 4 on Thursday, marking the completion of a two-year restoration project. The historic waterfront district, which has seen significant redevelopment in recent years, is expected to draw thousands of residents and tourists this weekend.

Evening walks reopen after dusk, and the market stalls below the promenade stay open an hour later to accommodate the expected crowds. Local business owners are hopeful that the return of the lights will signal a strong autumn season for the district.
